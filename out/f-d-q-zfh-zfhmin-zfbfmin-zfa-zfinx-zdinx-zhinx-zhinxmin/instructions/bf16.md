# FCVT.BF16.S, FCVT.S.BF16 — BF16 conversions

BF16 is the 16-bit format with $w_e=8$, $w_f=7$, $E_{\min}=-126$, $E_{\max}=127$; its exponent range equals that of S but its precision is lower. `FCVT.BF16.S` narrows and may be inexact, produce a BF16 subnormal, underflow to zero, or overflow; these cases take $\mathrm{exact}=x$, $\mathrm{rounded}=\mathrm{round}_{B,m}(\mathrm{exact})$. `FCVT.S.BF16` widens and is always exact. Both require `F` and `Zfbfmin`; the `impl(F)` conjunct is redundant because `Zfbfmin` implies `F`, but it is stated explicitly like the other format conditions.

The `exception` field of every block below lists only the exceptions the instruction itself can raise, and reads `\mathrm{exception}=\varnothing` when it raises none; the instruction-fetch outcomes depend only on the fetch address and are stated once in the README, not repeated here.

**(FCVT.BF16.S.0) Extension unavailable or effective FS Off → only IllegalInstruction(2)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\bigl(\bigl(\mathrm{Priv}\in\{\mathrm{VS},\mathrm{VU}\}\land(vsstatus.FS=\mathrm{Off}(0)\lor sstatus.FS=\mathrm{Off}(0))\bigr)\\
&\qquad\lor\ \neg\mathrm{impl}(\mathrm{F})\ \lor\ \neg\mathrm{impl}(\mathrm{Zfbfmin})\ \lor\ mstatus.FS=\mathrm{Off}(0)\bigr)\\
&\land\ (\mathrm{instr}=\mathrm{FCVT.BF16.S})\\
\end{aligned}
\longrightarrow
\{\, \mathrm{exception}=\{\mathrm{IllegalInstruction}(2)\} \,\}
$$
